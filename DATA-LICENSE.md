## 3. 署名方式 / How to attribute

最低要求：写明「新加坡 AI 观察 / sgai.md」，并链接到你所引用的具体页面（不是首页）。机读接口（`/data/*.json`、`.md` 导出、RSS）已在每条记录里带 `links.sgai` 或等价字段，直接使用即可。

Minimum: name "Singapore AI Observatory / sgai.md" and link to the specific page you drew from (not the homepage). Machine-readable surfaces (`/data/*.json`, `.md` exports, RSS) carry a `links.sgai` field or equivalent on every record — use that.

`/data/*.json`, `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `.md` exports, and RSS are anonymous and read-only; no API key. Respect `robots.txt` and response cache headers; do not mirror the whole site.
